# Couples Check-In Worksheet

Just a random side project — a small, self-contained HTML worksheet that couples can fill in together (or separately, then compare) to talk through the things that matter.

**Live page:** https://rdv21.github.io/couples-checkin-worksheet/

## What it is

A gentle set of questions across 10 areas: where you stand, communication & conflict, trust & boundaries, emotional connection, intimacy, time & independence, money & practical life, family & friends, values & the future, and checking in.

Each question includes a one-line note on why it's worth asking.

## Privacy

**No data is collected.** There is no backend, no analytics, no tracking, no network requests. Everything happens locally in your browser:

- Answers are saved only in your own browser's local storage.
- The **Save .txt** button downloads a file straight to your device.
- The **Load .txt** button reads that file back in — nothing is ever uploaded.

Nothing you type leaves your machine.

## How to use it

1. Open the live page (or the `index.html` file directly — it works fully offline).
2. Fill in the answers. They auto-save in your browser.
3. Click **Generate final version**, then **Copy** or **Save .txt** to keep or share it.
4. Come back anytime and use **Load .txt** to reopen a saved file and revise your answers.
